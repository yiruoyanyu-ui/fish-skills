# Candidate Taste Examples

These examples are hypotheses and few-shot material for `taste-rule-generator-prompt.md`, not a lookup table of verified rules. Small local reviewer observations must not become universal constraints. Promote a rule only after an authorized draft, visual review and user review; record its case and scope.

| Concept | Candidate visual approach | Risk to avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Noise reduction | Jagged sound lines becoming quieter/smoother at the product | Explosion particles misread as smoke |
| Water resistance | Droplets beading and rolling off | Unrequested underwater scene |
| Fast charging | Energy entering the actual charging interface | Lightning striking/damaging the product |
| Insulation | Scene-specific condensation or an external temperature boundary | Unrequested steam around a thermos |
| Light weight | A credible grip or floating composition with shadow | Arbitrary piles of feathers |
| Battery endurance | A coherent motion/light metaphor if justified | Invented performance figures |
| Natural ingredients | Actual supplied ingredients | Generic green gradient |
| Power | Controlled motion/cues from the real mechanism | Explosions implying loss of control |
| Connectivity | One or two meaningful connections | Cluttered network mesh |

Name real, tactile materials. Use one saturated focal element and subordinate supporting elements. Check recognition at approximately 120 pixels wide. Avoid stock orange/blue, purple/cyan neon, sparkle particles, excessive glow and empty "8K masterpiece" wording unless relevant to the brief.

Keep human-scale photography by default; internal, microscopic or space views require an educational brief. Exaggeration must preserve actual product capabilities and recognizable structure.
